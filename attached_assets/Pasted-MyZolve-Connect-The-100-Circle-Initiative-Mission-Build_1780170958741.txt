Circle Builder Responsibilities

Phase 1

Recruit 10–25 trusted members.

Examples:

* Family network
* Church ministry
* Neighborhood group
* Parent community
* Friend collective
* Professional network

⸻

Phase 2

Host one gathering.

Virtual or in person.

Purpose:

Introduce members to:

* the mission
* the circle
* the platform

⸻

Phase 3

Facilitate connection.

Examples:

* Resource sharing
* Community projects
* Skill exchanges
* Lending and borrowing
* Support requests
* Celebrations
* Volunteer opportunities

What Success Looks Like

Most startups measure:

* Downloads
* Views
* Likes

Connect should measure:

Community Health Metrics

* Active circles
* Members per circle
* Resource exchanges
* Support requests fulfilled
* Events hosted
* New relationships formed
* Dollars or services exchanged
* Member retention

Because if 100 circles each have 25 active members, that’s only 2,500 people.

But it’s 2,500 people connected through trust.

And that’s far more valuable than 25,000 random users scrolling a feed.
