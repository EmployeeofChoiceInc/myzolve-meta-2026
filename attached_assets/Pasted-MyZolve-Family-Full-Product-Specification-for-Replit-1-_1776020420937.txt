MyZolve Family

Full Product Specification for Replit

1. Product Overview

Product name: MyZolve Family

Position in ecosystem: A private support and exchange platform within the MyZolve ecosystem that helps families and trusted circles lend, borrow, repay, barter, organize support, and strengthen mutual aid.

Core idea:
MyZolve Family gives trusted groups their own private digital home where they can:
	•	request and provide financial support
	•	track repayment in a relationship-sensitive way
	•	exchange services and favors
	•	organize family resources
	•	support one another during normal life and difficult times
	•	optionally connect with other trusted circles later

This is not a public marketplace and not a traditional fintech product. It is a trust-based family support platform with financial and service-exchange functionality.

⸻

2. Product Philosophy

The product should feel:
	•	warm
	•	private
	•	supportive
	•	calm
	•	human
	•	non-punitive
	•	emotionally intelligent

The product should not feel:
	•	cold
	•	bank-like
	•	debt-collector-like
	•	socially shaming
	•	gamified in a way that harms relationships
	•	public or performative

Guiding principle

This platform should help people organize support without making relationships feel transactional.

⸻

3. Product Vision

MyZolve Family is designed to become the private infrastructure layer for families, chosen families, and trusted circles.

Long-term purpose

To help people rely on each other more clearly, more safely, and more sustainably.

Immediate purpose

To provide a private digital space where trusted people can coordinate money, services, needs, support, and repayment.

⸻

4. Core User Types

The product should support multiple forms of trusted circles, not just traditional families.

Supported circle types
	•	biological families
	•	blended families
	•	chosen families
	•	caregiver groups
	•	multigenerational households
	•	close friend circles
	•	faith-based support groups
	•	neighborhood support groups
	•	emergency support circles

Use “circle” as the primary universal term in the product.

⸻

5. Primary Jobs To Be Done

Users come to MyZolve Family to:
	•	ask for help without awkwardness
	•	lend money with more clarity
	•	pay people back without confusion
	•	document support that usually lives only in text messages or memory
	•	offer services to trusted people
	•	trade skills and favors
	•	reduce family tension caused by forgotten or vague agreements
	•	coordinate help during emergencies or temporary hardship
	•	maintain dignity while asking for support
	•	keep support networks organized

7.14 Soft Ledger

This is important, but it must be designed carefully.

The product should track support exchanged within a circle without making users feel scored.

Ledger items to track
	•	money given
	•	money received
	•	money repaid
	•	services offered
	•	services received
	•	gifted support
	•	forgiven amounts
	•	outstanding balances if allowed
	•	net support position if enabled

Ledger display options

Circle owners should choose one of these modes:

Mode 1: Private participant-only
Only participants in a transaction see the details.

Mode 2: Summary visible
Members can see general summaries but not exact details.

Mode 3: Full circle transparency
All circle members can see all support records.

Mode 4: Admin-only view
Only owner/admins can see the full ledger.

Suggested soft states

Instead of exposing a harsh balance score, the interface can display:
	•	You’ve supported your circle recently
	•	You’ve received support recently
	•	Your circle is active and responsive
	•	Several support exchanges have been completed

Full ledger fields
	•	ledger_entry_id
	•	circle_id
	•	entry_type

	•	source_entity_id
	•	giver_member_ref
	•	receiver_member_ref

7.16 Cross-Circle Exchange

Build the architecture now even if full functionality comes later.

This feature should allow trusted circles to optionally connect with other trusted circles for support and barter.

Important principle

Cross-circle access must be opt-in only.

Cross-circle use cases
	•	Family A needs tutoring; Family B offers it

Cross-circle requirements
	•	circle owners control whether cross-circle participation is allowed

	•	invitation or approved connection only
	•	visibility can be service-only, money-only, or full
	•	separate permission layer from internal circle permissions

14. Business Logic Rules

Request rules
	•	only authorized members can create requests
	•	circle settings determine who can post
	•	requests can be draft/private/open
	•	multiple contributors may fund one request
	•	gifts should not generate repayment unless manually changed
	•	loans generate repayment tracking if enabled
	•	a request may be closed manually or automatically when fully funded

Contribution rules
	•	contributor must be authorized
	•	amount cannot exceed remaining request amount unless overfunding is enabled
	•	overfunding behavior should be configurable:
	•	block
	•	allow with recipient confirmation
	•	convert extra to circle support pool later

Repayment rules
	•	repayment cannot exceed remaining owed unless allowed and explained
	•	forgiveness should update outstanding balance
	•	disputes should freeze status until resolved
	•	manual edits must be logged in audit trail

Service rules
	•	service matches can be accepted/declined
	•	a service post can spawn a barter agreement
	•	one service post may have multiple interested responders
	•	final match closes or partially reserves the post based on capacity settings

Visibility rules
	•	privacy settings always override feed visibility
	•	members only see what they are authorized to see
	•	hidden or sensitive requests should remain off global circle views except for participants/admins if permitted
