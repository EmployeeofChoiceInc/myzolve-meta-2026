## Standard "What's New" (App Store)

Lots of love in this update.

• A warmer app open: you'll now see a gentle peach reveal each time you
  launch MyZolve Connect.

• Care Calendar: plan rides, meals and visits together as a circle, with
  reminders so nothing falls through the cracks.

• Co-pilot for elders: post on behalf of someone in your circle who
  prefers a helping hand, with their approval.

• Gratitude wrap-ups: a quiet weekly and monthly recap of the care
  you've given and received.

• Voice notes and short videos: send a voice message or a quick clip on
  posts and requests (Plus).

• Verified circles: trusted groups now show a verified badge.

• Introducing MyZolve Connect Plus: unlimited circles, helper roles,
  Ledger Pro exports, voice notes, short videos, more photos, custom
  circle looks, and family plan support. $3.99/mo or $29.99/yr.

• A refreshed logo and a link out to MyZolve.com.

Thank you for trusting us with your circles. Keep the feedback coming.
